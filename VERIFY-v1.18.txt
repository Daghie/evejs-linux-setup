Known limits / not verified
---------------------------
- No EVE client login or gameplay through the launcher (would open the game).
- Not run on real SteamOS / Steam Deck hardware; SteamOS routing was forced on Fedora.
- Market profiles v2 and v3 were not seeded (they download live Tranquility data); their
  commands are the installer's existing ones.
- A data restore does not delete files created after the backup (for example a new
  config file); databases are restored together with their -wal/-shm state.
- Update backups include installed content packs (about 330 MB per backup); use
  "List or delete backups" / `prune` to keep the newest few.
